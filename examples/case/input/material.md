# A minimal fixture for the paper-translate pipeline

## Abstract

We measured the optical response of a two-dimensional crystal with a thickness of 3 layers.
The band gap was found to be 1.29 eV, which is 0.6 eV larger than the bulk value [12].
Photoluminescence was not observed at 10 K in samples grown on a silicon substrate (Fig. 2a).

## Results

Raman spectroscopy showed a linewidth of 2.4 cm-1 for the monolayer, and the sample was only
stable below 350 K. In contrast to earlier work [7, 8], we did not detect any measurable
exciton binding energy shift.
